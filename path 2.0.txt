Le path se debloque APRES avoir battue le 2 iem boss a la win 50, elle debloque deux choix de class, remplace le move 1 et 2 avec les nouveau de la classe Path et rename le defensive skill. Il remplace aussi le passive de la classe de base (pas celui de la multiclasse) si tu avais specialized tu recois le new passive x 2. Path debloque aussi un tab Journey, journey a une bare d'exp a elle meme, elle a un max de trentre niveau, journey eslt la ou tu retrouve les paths skill, aller j'usquau niv 15 est relativement rapide alors que de 15-30 est relativement lent, tu recois 1 journey skill par niveau de journey, ils servent a acheter les path skills. Finalement path augmente de 50% le scaling du combined(multiclass) scaling AINSI que le hp scaling.

RANGER PATHS

RANGER
Path A — Deadeye (Enhanced Ranger):
Amplifies what Ranger already does — bleed and multi-hit.

New Passive — Bloodhound: Predator heal doubles when enemy is bleeding. Rewards keeping bleed up constantly.
New Moves:

Piercing Shot: Physical, high power, ignores 30% of enemy DEF. Scales ATK.
Rain of Arrows: 3 hits, lower power per hit, applies bleed on last hit.
Replaces Quick Shot and Bleed Arrow


Path Skills:

Bleed deals +3% more damage per point (max 10)
Predator heals +2% more per point (max 10)
Rain of Arrows fires +1 extra arrow (10 points, max 1) — all-in skill



Path B — Ghost (Transformed Ranger):
Flips identity completely — forget bleed, become untouchable. AGI becomes everything.

New Passive — Phantom Step: Every evade permanently gives +1 AGI, heals on eavade 10% max hp
New Moves:

Shadowstep: Def type, scales on agi instead of def+mnd
Phantom Arrow: Damage scales purely on AGI instead of ATK



Path Skills:

+2% Agi per point (max 10)
On evade deal 40% ATK back as counter (max 10, +4% per point)
Phantom Arrow +1 shot (10 points, max 1)
